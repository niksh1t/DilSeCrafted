Our Story

Sab kuch shuru hua ek choti si mitti ki butterfly se. Ek din maine sirf shauk mein clay uthaya, aur usse ek butterfly banayi – aur us moment mein mujhe samajh aaya, ki creativity ke raaste par chalna hai. Woh butterfly sirf ek art piece nahi thi, woh meri journey ka pehla kadam tha.

Mainne alag-alag materials ke saath experiment kiya – kabhi kuch tikta nahi, kabhi kuch tut jaata tha. Par har baar maine seekha, aur har baar main aur behtar bani. Finally, maine woh material dhoondh nikala jo strong bhi tha aur sundarta bhi barkaraar rakhta tha.

Fir maine apna pehla stall lagaya – aur wahi se Dil Se Crafted ka safar shuru hua. Har piece jo main banati hoon, woh sirf ek jewellery item nahi hota – woh meri feelings, mera passion aur mera art hota hai. Yeh brand mere dil se nikla hai, isliye iska naam rakha – Dil Se Crafted.

Aaj bhi jab koi mere haathon ka bana piece pehenta hai, toh lagta hai jaise mera ek chhota sa hissa unke saath chala gaya hai – pyaar, mehnat aur creativity ke saath.
